# Dot Product

The hero detail uses a row of integer inputs and a small matrix of integer weights. Each row ends in the signed sum of its products. The drawing has the scale of a printed swatch. It suggests the arithmetic inside an installed model without presenting invented runtime telemetry.

The rows share a periodic bias and a seeded offset. That gives the weights a diagonal rhythm with occasional breaks. Filled and open marks distinguish positive and negative weights. Zero leaves a gap in the weave.

Each output bar starts at the same center line. Its direction follows the sign of the sum and its length follows its magnitude. Some busy rows produce short bars because their products cancel. Others produce longer marks even when the individual weights are small.

The detail fits between the installed model and the example chat. It draws once with a fixed seed so the pattern stays the same across visits. Wide spacing and a limited palette keep the smallest marks clear on a phone.
